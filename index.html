Technical report v1.1.0 · 2026 · Not peer reviewed

Experience allocation in blind quadruped obstacle learning

Can harder obstacle skills be acquired without losing ordinary locomotion? With Deepan Muthirayan, I compared two task-allocation strategies under a shared continuation budget and examined both climbing and retention.
